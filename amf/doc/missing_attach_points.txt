SUMMARY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
- amf_functions.c today only attaches to internal/sbi/processor.(*Processor).*
  (SBI northbound service callbacks) -- a completely separate surface.
- 0 of the 23 PDF state hook points (Parts 1-2) are present in amf_functions.c.
- The only PDF-listed hook already implemented anywhere is the XDP
  SCTP/NGAP PPID=60 ingress probe, done separately in amf/xdp_ngap.c.
- To instrument the threat-aware FSM, new attach_target arrays are needed
  for packages: internal/gmm, internal/gmm/message, internal/gmm/common,
  internal/ngap, internal/ngap/message, internal/nas,
  internal/nas/nas_security, internal/context, internal/sbi/consumer
  (namfService, nausfService, nudmService, npcfService, nsmfService,
  nssfService).
